# Surface brief: promo page (docs/index.html)

Scope: one static page for GitHub Pages, plain HTML and CSS plus a little JavaScript; visitor mode Persuade. Audience: students reading English course books. Action: install (Install and GitHub in the header, "How to install" under the opening; the commands, with Copy buttons, in Try it). Proof: six real screenshots (docs/img), measured numbers linked to their sources; nothing invented, no author name, no empty video or slides slots. Content: designs/promo-page.md, sections 1-9.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is sentmine at work, not a page about an app: the reader sees a real study question from the book, retyped large and clickable (and said to be retyped), the word in yellow, its card, and that card coming back twelve pages later, before reading any claim. The real PDF page appears in the step screenshots. It refuses the split hero over a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: sentmine's own interface: paper ground #f6f5f1, white panels with #d9d6cd hairlines, ink #1f2328, action blue #1a5fd0, picked-word yellow #ffe27a, dotted blue underline for words that come back; system UI for interface text, Georgia for the book.

STORY: The visitor sees a word clicked on a real page become a card, follows that one card through meaning, cloze, Anki and its return in a later chapter, believes it because every image is a real run and every number has a source, and installs it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The app header (sentmine; How it works, Comes back, Try it; GitHub and a blue Install). Under it the headline in two beats, "Click a word, get an Anki card. Meet the word again, and your card comes back.", at display size (larger than every section heading) with one line beneath. Below, the page-13 study question retyped large in Georgia, every word clickable, "simultaneously" in yellow; under it, side by side, the card it made and "Page 25, twelve pages later": the word underlined with the card it already has pointing up at it. A one-line note under them says the card backs use sentmine's own base-form rules, not always right. Install is the primary action, top right and repeated in Try it.

FORM: "The page is the reader", 1 on my ordered list of 7, dealt and locked by the user; signature interaction: one card carried through the five steps, the panel staying in view while the steps scroll past (raised from the origami sequence: one continuous object, never five tiles). seed:3c8b71f11ef89147
