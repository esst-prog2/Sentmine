# Timing spike: a card by hand, and with sentmine

How long does one card take, by hand in Anki and with sentmine? The answer goes on slide 3 of the demo-day slides
("I timed it myself").

## The card

Both ways make the same card:

- front: the word;
- back: its whole sentence with the word in **bold**, and a short meaning under it.

## What to do

1. Open the solution manual of *Causal Inference in Statistics: A Primer* on pages you have not used for cards.
   Pick **10 words** you do not know well. Write them in the table below before you start.
2. Make the cards **in turns**: word 1 by hand, word 2 with sentmine, word 3 by hand, and so on, so neither way
   gets the benefit of practice.
3. Time each card with a phone stopwatch, from the moment you have found the word on the page until the card is
   in Anki.
   - **By hand:** copy the sentence from the PDF, open Anki's Add window, type the word on the front, paste the
     sentence on the back, make the word bold, look up a meaning (a dictionary, or a search) and type it in,
     press Add, and go back to your page.
   - **With sentmine:** click the word, press Suggest meaning, read the meaning (edit it if it is wrong), and press
     Send to Anki.
4. Write the seconds in the table. If something goes wrong (a slip, a phone call), write it in the note column
   and time that word again.
